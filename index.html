ABOUT CASENET
PUBLIC CASE TRANSPARENCY NETWORK · PROTOTYPE · v1.0-PROTO
MISSION

CASENET is a public-facing aggregation and visualization tool built to increase transparency around missing persons, unidentified remains, and unsolved homicides in the United States. Every case on this platform represents a real person — a family waiting for answers, an investigation that deserves public attention.

This tool is not a law enforcement system. It does not replace official databases or investigative processes. Its purpose is to surface public-record information in a format that is accessible, searchable, and useful to families, journalists, researchers, and the general public.

DATA SOURCES
NamUs
namus.gov
National Missing and Unidentified Persons System. Federal database operated by the Department of Justice. Source for missing persons and unidentified remains, via manually-exported public search results.
Washington Post
github.com/washingtonpost/data-homicides
"Murder with Impunity" dataset. Homicide records across 50 major US cities, 2007–2017. Historical, not live — refreshed weekly on this site but the underlying records are not updated by WaPo.
MAP
murderdata.org
Murder Accountability Project. Non-profit compiling unsolved homicide data from FBI and coroner records, including case linkages formally noted in official records by law enforcement or MAP's own published research — not a CASENET-generated analysis.
FBI CDE
cde.ucr.cjis.fbi.gov
FBI Crime Data Explorer. Aggregated violent crime statistics by agency and jurisdiction. Used for regional context and clearance rate baselines.
※ Missing-persons, unidentified-remains, and homicide data are generated by an automated weekly pipeline from the sources above (see the SYNC line in the header for this session's data status). Any case type without a live export loaded falls back to illustrative mock data — check the CIRCUMSTANCES field and case ID prefix if you're unsure which you're looking at.
HOW TO USE THIS TOOL
01
MAP VIEW
Browse all cases geographically. Filter by type, date range, or search by name, city, or state. Click any dot to open the case detail panel. Zoom in to street level. Toggle the geo-temporal pattern overlay to surface cases with official agency-noted links.
02
CASE INDEX
Full sortable table of all cases. Click any column header to sort. Use the sidebar filters to narrow results. Click a row to fly to that case on the map. Export the current filtered dataset as a CSV for offline research.
03
ANALYTICS
Charts and statistics derived from the current filtered dataset. Explore case volume over time, breakdown by type and status, cases by state, and lead agency data. All charts update live as filters change.
04
SUBMITTING TIPS
CASENET does not collect tips directly. Use the "Submit Tip to Agency" button on any case to contact the lead agency. Never post speculation publicly — unverified information can harm investigations and families.
RESPONSIBLE USE
FOR RESEARCHERS & JOURNALISTS

All data is sourced from public records. Attribution to the originating database (NamUs, MAP, FBI CDE) is required when publishing. Do not republish case photographs or personally identifiable information beyond what appears here. Contact the lead agency before publishing details of active investigations.

FOR FAMILIES

If you have information about a case, contact the lead agency listed on the case record directly. If you believe your family member's case is missing from this database, contact NamUs at 1-833-526-2887 to submit or update a record. CASENET is not a substitute for working directly with law enforcement.

ABOUT PATTERN FLAGS

The geo-temporal pattern overlay surfaces cases where law enforcement or the Murder Accountability Project has formally noted potential geographic or temporal linkage in official case records. These flags are not CASENET's own algorithmic inference — CASENET does not compute or generate its own linkage analysis; it only displays a linkage that was already noted in the source record. Showing speculative pattern connections publicly can compromise investigations, harm innocent people, and re-traumatize families. If you believe cases are linked, contact the lead agencies directly.

LEGAL & LIMITATIONS

CASENET is a prototype research and transparency tool. It is not an official law enforcement system, not affiliated with any government agency, and does not have access to restricted law enforcement databases such as ViCAP. Case information may be incomplete, outdated, or contain errors inherited from source databases. Always verify information directly with the lead agency before acting on it.
